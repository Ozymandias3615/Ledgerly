Privacy Policy

This explains what Ledgerly collects across the desktop app and LedgerlyGo, why, and what control you have over it. Written in plain language on purpose — no filler.

Effective August 6, 2026

The short version

Ledgerly is free and has no ads, no analytics trackers, and no data broker relationships — we don't sell or rent your data to anyone, period. What we collect exists solely to run the features you use: your books, invoices, inventory, and (if you turn it on) AI features. You can export everything or delete your account yourself, any time, from Settings.

What we collect

Only what you give us by using the app:

  • Account info — email, name, and either a hashed password (if you sign up with email) or your Google account identity (if you sign in with Google/Firebase). We never see or store your Google password.
  • Business info — business name, primary currency, an optional logo, invoice reminder settings, and (if you choose to add one) your own Groq API key for unlimited AI use.
  • The records you create — transactions, invoices, clients & vendors, inventory items, employees and payroll runs, and receipt photos you capture or upload.
  • Team data — invite codes and member roles, if you run a multi-person business.
  • AI conversations — if you use AI Insights, your chat messages and the financial summary sent to generate a reply are stored so your conversation history persists.
  • Push notification subscription — only if you opt in, so we know where to deliver alerts like invoice reminders or low-stock warnings.
  • Basic request logs — standard web server logs (IP address, timestamps) generated automatically by our hosting infrastructure, the same as any web service.

What we don't collect

No analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising pixels, no cross-site cookies, no behavioral profiling. The only cookie-equivalent in play is the session token that keeps you signed in.

Who we share it with

We use a small set of infrastructure providers to run Ledgerly. Each only receives the specific data it needs to do its job — none of them get everything, and none of them are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

How your data is used

To run the app: store and display your records, generate reports and exports, send you notifications you've opted into, and — only when you use those specific features — power AI receipt scanning and AI Insights. That's it. We don't use your financial data for advertising, and we don't build profiles from it.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Sessions use signed tokens that expire, and each business's data is scoped to its members — no cross-business access.

Your controls

  • Export everything — Settings gives you a one-click ZIP export of your transactions, invoices, clients, inventory, employees, and payroll as CSV files.
  • Delete your account — also self-serve, from Settings. If you're the sole owner of a business, deleting your account deletes that business's data with it; if others share the business, you'll be asked to transfer ownership or remove them first so their access isn't silently cut off.
  • Bring your own AI key — add your own free Groq API key in Settings to use AI features on your own quota instead of the shared one.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account (see above) removes it — for a sole-owned business, that removal is immediate and complete.

Children's privacy

Ledgerly is a business tool and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the effective date above. Since Ledgerly doesn't collect an email list or send notifications outside the app, checking back here is the way to stay current.
