Roadmap

Ledgerly started as a business bookkeeping app. Personal finances, mobile companion apps, and multiple businesses have all shipped since — here's what's live today. For what changed in each release, see the changelog.

Business & Personal, one account

Full business bookkeeping, or a separate Personal side for your own budgets, bills, and savings goals — pick one at signup, add the other anytime. Any account can also create additional businesses of its own, not just join others via invite code.
