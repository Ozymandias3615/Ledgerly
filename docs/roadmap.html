Looking ahead

Roadmap

Ledgerly today is built for running a business. Here's where things are headed — including a plan to split into a dedicated business app and a new app for personal finances. Nothing below is available yet except the first entry.

Now
Live

Ledgerly, for business

The app as it exists today: bookkeeping, invoices, inventory, payroll, reports, team roles, and AI insights — everything on the main site right now.

See current features →
Next

Split the brand

The current app is renamed Ledgerly Business — same product, same features, a name that reflects it's built for running a company rather than personal finances.

Later

Ledgerly Personal launches

A new, separate app for personal finances and budgeting — tracking your own spending, income, and savings goals, kept apart from any business's books.
